# 3D 解剖 Anatomy

An interactive 3D anatomy viewer for patient education, in Traditional Chinese and English. It runs entirely in the browser; nothing is uploaded and no personal data is stored (only display preferences in the browser).

- **全身 Body:** whole-body anatomy by system, with clinical views (internal medicine, cardiac conduction, ENT, nephrology, urology, orthopaedics, rehab, neurology and common conditions such as carpal tunnel syndrome, rotator cuff tear, herniated disc, plantar fasciitis and shingles dermatomes).
- **腦 Brain:** brain atlas with lobes, deep nuclei, ventricles and pathways.
- **Search (⌕ or the / key):** finds and highlights a muscle, organ, nerve, vessel or bone by English or Chinese name.

Works on phones, tablets and computers with a current browser (iOS 16.4 or later, recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).

> For patient education and teaching only. Not a medical device; not for diagnosis or treatment decisions.
